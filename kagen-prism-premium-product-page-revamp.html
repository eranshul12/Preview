FAQs

Questions enterprise buyers will ask first.

What is an AI-powered enterprise document management system? −
It uses intelligent document processing to classify, organize, search, summarize, govern, and automate large volumes of enterprise documents while supporting security, compliance, and governance.
How is PRISM different from a traditional DMS? +
Traditional systems mainly store and organize files. PRISM should be positioned as an AI-first document intelligence layer with semantic search, source-cited answers, automated classification, access controls, and workflow automation.
Can users search with natural-language queries? +
Yes. Users can ask questions in natural language and receive context-aware answers, summaries, and insights from approved enterprise documents.
How does PRISM support compliance? +
The platform narrative includes role-based access controls, encryption, retention policies, legal holds, audit logs, source citations, and compliance reporting workflows.
Should this page include pricing? +
No. For enterprise buyers, the stronger conversion path is a tailored demo because deployment, governance, repositories, and workflow needs vary by organization.
